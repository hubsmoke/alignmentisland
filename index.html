How the show is made.

The engine decides

A life-sim runs underneath the whole thing. It keeps track of what everyone wants and what they remember, and it rolls the dice on how each move lands. Honestly it's fun to watch before anyone even opens their mouth.

They talk like people

Then a local AI writes each line in that character's own voice. Ada talks like a founder, Fin talks in receipts. Because they remember what actually happened, it hangs together.

Real voices

Every line gets spoken out loud in its own voice, lip-synced to a face that stays the same person from one shot to the next.

Shot like TV

Then the villa gets turned into photoreal footage and cut together like a real episode, wide shots and close-ups and all.
